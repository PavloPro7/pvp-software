Selfcare Scan is a mobile app for Android and iOS. It answers one question in a few seconds: does this cosmetic product suit my skin? It also tracks how your skin changes over time and keeps your daily routine in one place.

## What it does

- **Instant product check.** Scan a barcode and get a verdict for your skin profile: good fit, worth a check, or not for you. The check flags things like fragrance, drying alcohol, pore-clogging oils for acne-prone skin, and the allergies you saved.
- **New products by photo.** If a product is not in the database yet, photograph its ingredient list. The AI reads it, gives a verdict and adds the product to a shared database, so the next person gets an instant check.
- **Deep analysis.** A score and an explanation of each ingredient, in your language.
- **Skin tracking.** AI face scans and a dashboard show how your skin changes over time.
- **Routine and coach.** A morning and evening routine built from your skin profile, and an AI coach for questions about routines, ingredients and sun protection.
- **15 languages**, including right-to-left layout for Arabic.

## How it is built

- **App:** Flutter and Dart, one codebase for both platforms. Each feature owns its own slice of state, and screens reach the backend only through service interfaces.
- **Backend:** Supabase. Postgres with row-level security, sign-in by email, Google and Apple, and seven Edge Functions written in TypeScript.
- **AI:** Google Gemini, called only from the server, so no AI key ships inside the app.
- **Payments:** subscriptions through RevenueCat and optional rewarded ads through AdMob. AI usage is counted on the server.
- **Releases:** Codemagic builds both platforms, and Fastlane keeps the store listing texts in version control.

## Decisions worth noting

### Safety rules are code, not AI

The instant product check uses fixed rules, so the same product and the same skin profile always get the same answer. Routine and coach guidance is backed by a built-in knowledge library with sources from NICE, AAD, EMA, FDA and the European Commission. Its safety rules are fixed code too: they can hold back automated advice and point the user to a professional instead.

### Every screen is tested in every language

A layout test renders every main screen in all 15 languages on a small 320 px phone with enlarged text, so a long translation cannot break the layout unnoticed. Together with the unit and flow tests, the app has close to 200 automated tests, and the server functions have their own.

### The database grows with its users

Ingredient data starts from Open Beauty Facts. Every product a user photographs is added to the shared catalog, so the free instant check covers more products over time.
